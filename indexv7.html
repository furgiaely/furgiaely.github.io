Understanding COE & PARF Rebates

Official COE Rebate Calculation Example (LTA)

Scenario: Car with COE that costs $38,000, expires on 5 June 2020, deregistered on 2 May 2012.

Unused COE Duration: From 2 May 2012 to 5 June 2020 = 8 years, 1 month, 3 days = 97.1 months

COE Rebate = ($38,000 × 97.1) ÷ 120 = $30,748

Key points:

  • 1 month = 30 days for calculation purposes
  • 3 days = 0.1 month (3 ÷ 30)
  • Total unused period = 97 months + 0.1 month = 97.1 months

COE Rebate Calculation (Official Method)

When you deregister your vehicle before the COE expires, you receive a rebate for the unused portion of your COE.

COE Rebate = (Original COE Price × Unused Months) ÷ 120

Where:

  • Original COE Price: The amount paid for the COE
  • Unused Months: Remaining period from deregistration to COE expiry (in decimal months)
  • 120: Total months in a 10-year COE period

Days to Months Conversion: For calculation purposes, 1 month = 30 days

Decimal Months = Whole Months + (Days ÷ 30)

No COE rebate is given after the full 10-year period.

PARF Rebate Calculation

The PARF (Preferential Additional Registration Fee) rebate is based on your car's OMV and age at deregistration:

Vehicle Age at Deregistration PARF Rebate (% of OMV)
Not exceeding 5 years 75% of OMV
Above 5 but not exceeding 6 years 70% of OMV
Above 6 but not exceeding 7 years 65% of OMV
Above 7 but not exceeding 8 years 60% of OMV
Above 8 but not exceeding 9 years 55% of OMV
Above 9 but not exceeding 10 years 50% of OMV
Above 10 years No PARF rebate
PARF Rebate = OMV × PARF Percentage

PARF rebate is only applicable for vehicles deregistered within 10 years of registration.

Total Rebate Calculation

Your total rebate upon deregistration is the sum of COE rebate (if any) and PARF rebate (if eligible).

Total Rebate = COE Rebate + PARF Rebate

For vehicles older than 10 years, only COE rebate may apply if deregistered early.

Important Considerations

  • COE rebate calculation uses 30 days per month for decimal conversion
  • PARF rebate is only for cars registered with COE
  • Minimum PARF rebate is 50% of OMV for cars under 10 years
  • Additional administrative fees may apply
  • Check with LTA for the most current rates and regulations
